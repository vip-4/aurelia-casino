How to play

  • Try to get a hand total closer to 21 than the dealer, without going over.
  • Number cards count as their face value; Jack, Queen, King = 10; Ace = 1 or 11.
  • Hit draws another card. Stand ends your turn.
  • Winning hand pays 2× your bet. A tie (push) returns your bet.
  • Dealer stands on 17 or higher and draws until reaching 17.

About this blackjack game

This free blackjack game is a lightweight, single-deck implementation that runs directly in your browser.
